# Compliance

Coming soon. These pages will explain, for each regulation, what MedBrains
does for you, what your hospital must still do, and where to find the
evidence:

- **ABDM:** ABHA creation and linking, consented record exchange, Scan & Share.
- **NABH indicators:** the quality indicators MedBrains measures and reports.
- **DPDP Act:** consent, patient rights requests, and audit of every PHI read.
- **NDPS and Schedule H/H1/X:** controlled-drug registers, witnesses and
  retained prescriptions. The [pharmacist quick guide](/user-guides/pharmacist/#controlled-drugs-ndps-schedule-x)
  covers the screens today.
- **CERT-In:** log retention and time sources.
