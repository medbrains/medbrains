# Register a patient

**Who:** receptionists and front-office staff.
**Where:** menu → **Patients** → **Register Patient**.

Registration is five steps: **Intake**, **Contact & identity**,
**Family · address · safety**, **Prefs & billing** and **Review**. The step
you are on is shown along the bottom of the screen.

## 1. Intake

![The intake step, filled in](/img/guide/register-1-filled.png)

1. Enter the patient's **name**. A patient with one name needs only the first
   name.
2. Enter the **age** or pick the **date of birth**. Typing an age gives an
   estimated date of birth of 1 January; pick the exact day in the calendar
   when you know it.
3. Choose the patient's **sex**. There is no default: it must be chosen.
4. Enter a **phone** number, or tick **Has no phone**. Many elderly patients
   have none; the record says so rather than holding a made-up number.
5. Under **Clinical ownership**, choose the **department** for the OPD visit,
   and the consultant if the patient asks for one.

For an unconscious or unidentified patient, tick **Unknown / unidentified
patient**: the name is skipped and the patient is known by their UHID.

## 2. Contact & identity

![The contact and identity step](/img/guide/register-2.png)

- **Agreed to receive:** ask the patient, and tick only what they agree to:
  hospital updates on WhatsApp or by email. SMS about their registration and
  visits needs no agreement.
- **ABHA** and **Aadhaar** are optional. Only a masked Aadhaar number is
  stored, never the full number.
- **Referred by:** who sent the patient — a doctor, an ASHA worker, a camp.

## 3. Family · address · safety

![The family, address and safety step](/img/guide/register-3.png)

The address, next of kin and emergency contact, and the safety flags: tick
**Medico-legal case** for an MLC (its MLC number is then required) and **VIP
patient** where the hospital's policy says so.

## 4. Prefs & billing

Allergies are recorded here: the patient's known allergies, and drug
allergies chosen from the hospital's drug list. Leave them empty only when
the patient was not asked — the review then says **Not asked yet**, and the
doctor sees a reminder before prescribing. Never leave a known allergy
unrecorded. This step also holds dietary and room preferences and billing
details.

## 5. Review

The review reads the record back before it is saved. Read it to the patient:
name, sex, date of birth, phone, department.

![The review step](/img/guide/register-review.png)

Select **Register complete**. When **Send to OPD queue after registration**
is ticked (on the first step), the patient is given a token and the OPD visit
opens.

:::tip Save now
**Save now** (on any step before Review) saves a patient with the minimum
details when the queue is long. Complete the record later from the patient's page.
:::

## When something goes wrong

- **Possible duplicates.** If the patient may already be registered, a list of
  close matches appears. Open the existing record if it is the same person;
  register a new one only when you have checked it is someone else.
- **Next does nothing.** A required field is missing; the message under the
  field says which.
- **No Patients menu, or no Register Patient button.** Your role cannot
  register patients; ask your hospital administrator.
