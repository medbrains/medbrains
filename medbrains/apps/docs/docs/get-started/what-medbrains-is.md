# What MedBrains is

MedBrains is software that runs a whole hospital: registration, OPD, wards,
pharmacy, lab, radiology, billing, HR and the reports management needs. One
record follows each patient through every department.

## What it is built to be

- **Cheap by design.** The software is free under the GNU AGPL-3.0 licence: no
  licence fee and no per-user fee. It runs a full hospital on one small
  server.
- **Top quality.** Safety rules such as drug interaction checks, allergy
  checks and controlled-drug registers are enforced by the server, not left
  to the screen. Every change and every look at a record is audited.
- **Out of the box.** The MedBrains OS box sets itself up on its own screen,
  with no command line. Staff, roles and modules are set up from the admin
  screens.
- **Works on any OS.** Use it in a browser on Windows, macOS or Linux, in the
  desktop app, on iPhone and Android, and on TV boards in the waiting area.
- **Regulator-ready.** Built for ABDM (ABHA, record linking and consented
  exchange), NABH quality indicators, the DPDP Act, NDPS and Schedule H/H1/X
  drug rules, and CERT-In logging and time-keeping.
- **Creates local jobs.** Because the code is public, local IT teams and firms
  can install, support and extend it for the hospitals near them.

## Who it is for

| You are | MedBrains gives you |
|---|---|
| A small or rural hospital | A complete hospital system on one small machine |
| A charitable or trust hospital | No software fees, so money goes to patients |
| A multi-specialty hospital | Many departments, branches and fine-grained roles |
| A developer or health-tech team | A real, tested hospital codebase to build on |
