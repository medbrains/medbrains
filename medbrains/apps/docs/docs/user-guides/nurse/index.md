# Nurse quick guide

**Who:** nurses in OPD and on the wards.

## Record OPD vitals

1. Go to **OPD Queue** → **Vitals counter**.
2. Select **Record vitals** on the patient.
3. Enter the readings. A reading out of range is marked **High** or
   **Critical high**.
4. Select **Record vitals**. The patient is now with the doctor.

## Record ward vitals

1. Go to **Nurse Activities** → **Vitals**.
2. Open the patient, choose **Record**, enter the values and select **Record
   vitals**. **Timeline** shows earlier readings; schedules show **Overdue**
   or **Due soon**.

## Give medicines (MAR)

1. Go to **Nurse Activities** → **MAR**.
2. On a due dose, select **Administer** and choose **Give**, **Hold**,
   **Refuse** or **Missed**.
3. Scan the **Patient wristband** and the **Drug barcode**, then select
   **Verify scan**.
4. Confirm the right dose, route and time. A high-alert drug needs a second
   nurse as **Witness**.
5. Select **Give dose**.

:::danger
"Barcode does not match — do not administer." Stop and check the patient and
the drug.
:::

Medicines the patient brought from outside show **Patient's own, brought
from outside**.

## Hand over your shift

1. Go to **Nurse Activities** → **Handoff**.
2. Pick the **Incoming nurse** and fill **Situation**, **Background**,
   **Assessment** and **Recommendation**.
3. Select **Sign handoff**. The incoming nurse selects **Accept**.

## Use the bed board

Go to **IPD** → **Bed Dashboard**. Filter by ward or status. On a bed, use
**Open admission**, **Transfer**, **Hold bed** or **Change status**.

## Write a medicine for the doctor to sign

With the prescription toggle on **Nurse**, you can add a line, including a
**Buy outside** line. It is held for the doctor and sent with **Send to
doctor**. See [mark buy outside](/user-guides/doctor/#mark-a-medicine-buy-outside).
