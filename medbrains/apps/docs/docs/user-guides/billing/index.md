# Billing quick guide

**Who:** billing staff and cashiers. **Where:** **Billing**.

## Create a bill

1. Select **New Invoice**.
2. Pick the patient and the **Visit**, then select **Create Draft Invoice**.
3. On the invoice, use **Add Item** for each charge.
4. Select **Issue**.

## Collect a payment

1. Select **Cashier counter**.
2. Find the **Patient** and set your **Counter**.
3. Under **Open bills**, select **Collect** on one bill, or collect all.
4. Choose the **Way of paying**: **Cash**, **Card**, **UPI QR**, **Payment
   link**, **Online**, **Deposit** or **Insurance / credit**. **Add another
   way** splits the payment.
5. Select **Collect**. The bill shows **Paid** or **Partly paid**.

If a payment shows **Not collected**, select **Collect again**: it is not
charged twice.

## Print a receipt

After collecting, select **Reprint** on the receipt. Later, use **Reprint
receipt** on the invoice. **Print packet** prints the invoice; it is greyed
out until the invoice is issued.

## Give a discount or concession

1. On the invoice, select **Add Discount**, choose the **Type** and
   **Value**, and select **Apply Discount**.
2. Concessions (charity, staff, camp, senior citizen and others) are approved
   in **Concessions** by someone with approval rights.

## Refund or credit

Go to **Refunds & Credits**. Use **Create Refund**, **Create Credit Note** or
**Request Write-Off**. A write-off waits for approval. An online payment is
refunded from its payment row with **Refund to the payer**.

## Close the day

1. Go to **Day Close** and select **Create day close**.
2. Enter the date, counter and shift, and the note counts. **Counted cash**
   adds itself up.
3. Enter card and UPI settled amounts, then submit.
4. A supervisor selects **Verify** and signs off.
