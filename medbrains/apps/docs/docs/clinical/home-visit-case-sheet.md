# Home visit case sheet

**Who:** the nurse sent on the visit; doctors and nurses with home-care access.

**Where:** menu → **Home visits**.

Every home visit has its own case sheet, like a clinic visit. Notes written
there belong to that visit and appear in the patient's record.

## Write and sign a note

1. Open **Home visits** and find the patient's row for the day.
2. Select **Case sheet**. The visit's case sheet opens beside the round.
3. Select **New note** and choose the note type: nursing note, progress note,
   dietitian note or physiotherapy note. A clinic consultation is not offered
   on a home visit.
4. Write the note. It saves itself as a draft; **Draft saved** shows when it
   has.
5. Select **Sign**. The note now carries your name and the time.

## See past home notes

Open **Home healthcare** and choose the patient. Their notes from every
visit, clinic and home, are listed together; home visits are labelled
**Home visit**.

## When something goes wrong

- **The visit's case sheet does not open.** Only the nurse sent on the visit,
  and staff with access to the patient, can open it. Ask whoever schedules
  the round to assign you to the visit.
- **No Home visits menu.** Your role does not have home-care access; ask
  your hospital administrator.
