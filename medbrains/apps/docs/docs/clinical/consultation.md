# Write and sign a consultation

**Who:** doctors.
**Where:** menu → **OPD Queue** → the patient → **Consultation**.

Every OPD visit has one case sheet. The consultation is written there, as a
note with four parts: **Subjective**, **Objective**, **Assessment** and
**Plan**.

## Write the note

1. Open the patient's visit and choose **Consultation** on the left.
2. Select **New note** → **Consultation**. Other kinds of note are offered
   too: a consult opinion, a history and physical, a progress note, a
   procedure note.
3. Write each part. The note saves itself as you type; **Draft saved** shows
   when it has. A draft is yours until you sign it.

## Sign it

Select **Sign**. The note now carries your name, your registration number,
your department and the time.

![A signed consultation on the case sheet](/img/guide/case-sheet-signed.png)

## After signing

- **Revise**: for a short while after signing (the hospital sets how long),
  you can correct the note. The signed version is kept.
- **Add addendum**: after that, add to the note rather than change it.
- **Entered in error**: when a note was written on the wrong patient or visit.
  Give the reason; the note stays on the record, struck through, so the
  record shows what happened.
- **PDF** prints the case sheet and files it to the visit's medical record.

## When something goes wrong

- **Consultation is not in the New note menu.** Your access to consultation
  notes is restricted; ask your hospital administrator.
- **"Content withheld" on a note.** The same restriction: you can see that a
  consultation exists, not what it says.
- **A resident's note waits for countersigning.** Notes by doctors you
  supervise appear under your countersign list until you countersign or
  return them with a comment.
