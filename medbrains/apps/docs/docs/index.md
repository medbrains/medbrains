# MedBrains Help

This guide explains how to do your day's work in MedBrains, screen by screen.

- **Getting started** covers signing in and finding your way around.
- **Front desk** covers registering patients and starting their visits.
- **Clinical** covers the case sheet and ordering tests.
- **Laboratory** covers an order from collection to verified report.

Each page says who does the task, where to find it in the menu, the steps,
and what to do when something goes wrong. You only see the menus and buttons
your role allows; if a page describes something you cannot find, ask your
hospital administrator for access.

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>) to search.
