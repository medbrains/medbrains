# SOP: Insurance & TPA

## Overview
The Insurance module covers the full insurance and Third-Party Administrator (TPA) workflow: eligibility verification at admission, pre-authorisation request, claim preparation and submission, settlement reconciliation, and denial management. It integrates with Billing (charges), IPD (admission details), and Lab/Pharmacy/Radiology (supporting documents). India-specific compliance: IRDAI regulations, NHA/NHCX claim interchange standard, CGHS/ECHS empanelment, PM-JAY / Ayushman Bharat.

## Actor Matrix

| Actor (Role) | Can Perform | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `insurance_officer` | Eligibility check, pre-auth, claim submission, settlement | Primary insurance actor |
| `billing_clerk` | Generate insurance-adjusted invoice, collect patient co-pay | Finance interface |
| `receptionist` | Capture insurance details at registration | Data entry at front desk |
| `hospital_admin` | Approve discounts, view TPA settlement reports | Senior approver |

## Scenario 1: Insurance Officer Verifies Eligibility at Admission — Actor: Insurance Officer

**Actor**: `insurance_officer`  
**Entry point**: Insurance module → Eligibility Verification (or triggered from IPD admission)  
**Preconditions**: Patient presenting for admission; TPA / insurance details provided by patient

**Steps**:
1. Insurance officer opens eligibility check; enters patient's TPA ID card number / policy number / Ayushman Bharat ID.
2. System queries TPA panel (via configured API or manual check if no integration): verifies policy active, coverage type, sum insured balance, network empanelment status of the hospital.
3. If integrated (NHCX / TPA API): eligibility response received in real-time.
4. Records eligibility result: covered (with sub-limits) / not covered / requires pre-auth.
5. Communicates to patient: covered amount, co-pay obligation, and any exclusions (e.g., pre-existing conditions under waiting period).
6. Attaches eligibility record to patient's IPD admission.

**Exit / Outcome**: Eligibility verified and documented; patient informed of coverage; pre-auth triggered if required.  
**Regulatory note**: IRDAI (TPA) Regulations 2016 — TPA must respond to pre-auth within 6 hours; Ayushman Bharat scheme — cashless treatment at empanelled hospitals; CGHS — eligibility verified via CGHS beneficiary database.

## Scenario 2: Insurance Officer Submits Pre-Auth and Manages Approval — Actor: Insurance Officer

**Actor**: `insurance_officer`  
**Entry point**: Insurance → Pre-Auth Requests → New Request  
**Preconditions**: Eligibility verified; IPD admission confirmed; doctor has documented provisional diagnosis

**Steps**:
1. Insurance officer opens new pre-auth request; links to IPD admission.
2. Fills pre-auth form: patient demographics, policy number, provisional diagnosis (ICD-10), proposed procedure, estimated treatment cost, estimated LOS, room type.
3. Attaches supporting documents (generated in-system): admission note, initial investigation reports.
4. Selects TPA from configured panel; form auto-formats to TPA's required template (or NHCX standard).
5. Submits; status → `submitted_to_tpa`.
6. TPA responds (NHCX callback / email / portal):
   - **Approved**: records sanctioned amount, authorisation number; updates IPD billing limit.
   - **Query**: TPA has questions — insurance officer responds with additional documents.
   - **Denied**: records reason; patient notified; billing mode changed to self-pay or alternate.
7. For enhancement (if treatment extends beyond initial auth): files enhancement request.

**Exit / Outcome**: Pre-auth outcome documented; sanctioned amount on IPD billing screen; denial documented with reason.  
**Regulatory note**: IRDAI — cashless pre-auth TAT: 1 hour (emergency), 6 hours (planned admission); NHCX standard for claim interchange; PM-JAY — online pre-auth via Ayushman Bharat portal.  
**Existing test**: `— needs test` (referenced in 07-S2 as a cross-module test)

## Scenario 3: Insurance Officer Submits Final Claim and Reconciles Settlement — Actor: Insurance Officer

**Actor**: `insurance_officer`  
**Entry point**: Insurance → Claims → New Claim (post-discharge)  
**Preconditions**: Patient discharged; final bill generated by billing clerk; all documents available

**Steps**:
1. Insurance officer opens final claim against the admission.
2. Attaches: discharge summary, final bill itemisation, lab/pharmacy/radiology reports, consent forms, pre-auth approval letter.
3. Generates claim package per TPA/NHCX format; submits.
4. Claim status → `submitted`; tracks TAT (IRDAI: final settlement within 30 days of last document).
5. TPA processes: sends settlement advice with approved and denied line items.
6. Insurance officer reviews settlement:
   - Approved: records payment received; reconciles against final bill.
   - Short-paid/denied items: raises deficiency letter or appeals.
7. Patient's co-pay / denied portion billed and collected.
8. Outstanding claims aged report monitored weekly.

**Exit / Outcome**: Claim settled; denied items tracked for appeal; patient co-pay collected; revenue cycle closed.  
**Regulatory note**: IRDAI (Health Insurance) Regulations 2016 — settlement within 30 days; NHCX — digital claim submission; CGHS — settlement via Treasury/direct to hospital.

## Scenario 4: Billing Clerk Adjusts Bill for TPA Settlement and Collects Co-Pay — Actor: Billing Clerk

**Actor**: `billing_clerk`  
**Entry point**: Billing → IPD Bill → Insurance Settlement  
**Preconditions**: TPA settlement advice received; insurance officer has recorded approved amount

**Steps**:
1. Billing clerk opens IPD final bill; sees "Insurance Approved: ₹X" line from insurance officer's entry.
2. System calculates patient co-pay: Total Bill − Insurance Approved Amount − Pre-paid Advance.
3. Billing clerk presents breakdown to patient/attendant: what insurance covers, what patient owes.
4. Collects co-pay via cash / card / UPI.
5. Issues final receipt: two lines — insurance settlement portion (pending transfer) + patient payment (collected).
6. Issues "No Dues Certificate" to patient; patient clears for discharge.
7. Insurance settlement (transfer from TPA) recorded separately when received.

**Exit / Outcome**: Patient co-pay collected; No Dues issued; insurance settlement tracked as receivable.  
**Regulatory note**: Consumer Protection Act 2019 — itemised bill mandatory; IRDAI — cashless means patient pays only co-pay/non-covered items; any balance billing dispute resolved per IRDAI ombudsman mechanism.
