# Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

MedBrains is dual-licensed (AGPL-3.0 + commercial — see [COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md](./COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md)). To keep that model legally sound, the project must hold the rights to license **all** contributed code under **both** licenses. This CLA grants those rights while letting you keep your copyright.

## Summary (plain language)

By contributing to MedBrains, you agree that:

1. **You keep your copyright.** You are not assigning ownership of your work.
2. **You grant the project a broad license.** You grant the MedBrains maintainers a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to use, reproduce, modify, sublicense and distribute your contribution **under both the AGPL-3.0 and the commercial license**. This is what allows the project to offer a commercial option that funds the open development.
3. **You have the right to contribute it.** The work is yours to give — it's your original work, or you have permission to submit it, and it doesn't knowingly infringe anyone's rights. If your employer has rights to work you create, you have permission to contribute.
4. **No warranty.** You provide your contribution "as is", without warranties.

## How you accept

We use the **Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO)** as the lightweight mechanism plus this CLA's licensing grant:

- Sign off every commit with `git commit -s`, which appends:
  `Signed-off-by: Your Name <your@email>`
- On your **first** pull request, a CLA-assistant bot will ask you to confirm acceptance of this agreement by commenting once. It records your acceptance against your GitHub account, so you only do it once.

The full Developer Certificate of Origin v1.1 text is at <https://developercertificate.org/>.

> If your organisation requires a signed paper CLA instead of the bot flow, contact the maintainers (see [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md)) and we'll arrange it.

Thank you — this small step is what keeps MedBrains both **free forever under AGPL** and **financially sustainable**.
