If you have changed doctors or moved cities, you know the problem: your reports
are in a dozen places — a file at home, a WhatsApp chat, a lab's website. The
**Ayushman Bharat Health Account (ABHA)** is built to fix that.

## What ABHA is

ABHA is a free, 14-digit health ID issued under the Ayushman Bharat Digital
Mission (ABDM). You can also pick an easy-to-remember ABHA address, like
`name@abdm`. It is voluntary.

## What it does for you

- **Your records can follow you.** Hospitals, clinics and labs that are linked to
  ABDM can connect your prescriptions, reports and discharge summaries to your
  ABHA.
- **You see them in one place**, in an ABDM-linked health app on your phone.
- **Faster registration.** Many hospitals can register you by scanning a QR code
  instead of filling in forms again.

## You decide who sees what

Nobody can pull your records just because they know your ABHA number. A doctor
or hospital has to **ask for your consent**, and you choose which records, for
how long, and you can withdraw that consent.

## How to get one

Go to the official site, abha.abdm.gov.in, and sign up with Aadhaar or another
supported method. Only use the official government site or apps listed on it,
and never share an OTP with anyone who calls you about your health ID.

## What it means for hospitals

For a hospital, ABDM means fewer repeat tests, faster registration and records
that arrive complete. It also means building consent and privacy into everyday
software — which is how we build MedBrains. Hospitals can also earn an
incentive for linking records to ABHA: our
[ABDM incentive calculator](/resources/abdm-incentive-calculator) estimates a
month's payment, and [PM-JAY, ABDM and DHIS in numbers](/resources/indian-hospitals-in-numbers)
shows how many ABHA IDs exist today.
