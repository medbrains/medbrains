The goal is simple: when a hospital, patient or buyer sees the name MedBrains, they should be able to trust that it is MedBrains, or clearly told that it is not.

## You may, without asking

- **Say what you run.** "Our hospital runs MedBrains", "we install and support MedBrains", "we offer MedBrains hosting", in plain text.
- **Redistribute it unchanged** under the name MedBrains, including the logo as it ships in the code, provided you do not change the software.
- **Name a modified fork honestly.** Give it your own name and say it is "based on MedBrains" or "a fork of MedBrains". The words "based on MedBrains" must not be larger or more prominent than your own product name.
- **Write about it.** Articles, talks, tutorials, reviews, research papers and comparisons may use the name to refer to the project.
- **Link to us** with the name as the link text.

## You need written permission to

- **Use the name in a product, service or company name**, such as "MedBrains Cloud", "MedBrains Pro" or "MedBrains for Clinics", or in a name that could be confused with it.
- **Call a modified version "MedBrains"**, or ship it with the MedBrains logo.
- **Claim a status**: "official", "certified", "authorised partner", "endorsed", "MedBrains Compatible" or anything that suggests we have checked or approved your product or service.
- **Use the logo in your own marketing**: websites, brochures, ads, slides, stalls or merchandise.
- **Register a domain, social media handle or app-store name** that contains "MedBrains" or something confusingly similar.
- **Register the name or logo** as a trademark, design or company name anywhere.

## Patient safety comes first

A hospital system that carries the MedBrains name is assumed to have its safety checks. If you change clinical rules, drug checks, access control or audit logging, do not present the result as MedBrains. Use your own name, so a hospital knows whom to ask.

## How to ask

Contact the maintainer through the address in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md), with the subject `TRADEMARK: MedBrains`. Say who you are, what you want to use (name, logo, or both), where, and for how long. We aim to answer within 10 business days. Permission is given in writing, may carry conditions, and can be withdrawn if those conditions are not met.

## Misuse

If you see the name or logo used in a way that misleads people, please tell us through the same address.
