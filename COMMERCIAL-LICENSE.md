## When you need a commercial license

The AGPL-3.0 requires that if you **modify** MedBrains and make it available to others **over a network** (i.e. run it as a hosted/SaaS service), you must offer those users the complete corresponding source code of your modified version, under the AGPL.

That obligation is healthy for the commons but unworkable for some organisations. **You likely need a commercial license if you:**

- want to offer a hosted MedBrains-based service **without** publishing your modifications,
- want to embed MedBrains inside a **closed-source** commercial product,
- need to combine it with proprietary code under terms incompatible with the AGPL,
- require a warranty, indemnity, SLA, or formal support contract.

**You do _not_ need a commercial license if you:**

- self-host MedBrains (modified or not) for your own hospital/organisation and don't offer it as a service to third parties, **or**
- run it as a service and are happy to publish your modifications under the AGPL, **or**
- are a public hospital, NGO, or government health programme using it under the AGPL.

> The AGPL is always available to everyone at no cost. The commercial license is an **alternative**, not a restriction on the open-source version.

## What the commercial license provides

- A licence to use MedBrains without the AGPL's network-copyleft source-sharing obligation.
- Optional support, SLAs, and security-update guarantees.
- Optional indemnification.

Revenue from commercial licenses funds continued open development and the public-good roadmap in [GRANTS.md](./GRANTS.md).

## How to obtain one

Open a private conversation via the contact in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md), or email the maintainers (see the repository owner's profile). Tell us roughly your deployment shape (hospitals served, hosted vs embedded, support needs) and we'll propose terms.
